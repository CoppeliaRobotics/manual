Measurable objects

Measurable objects are scene objects that can be used for minimum distance calculation against other measurable objects. They include:

  • Dummies
  • Shapes
  • OC trees
  • Point clouds

    • Measurable objects can have their measurable property individually enabled or disabled. This can be set in the scene object common properties or through sceneObject.measurable.

    Collections are also measurable, since they might contain measurable objects.

    Additionally, measurable objects can have their measurable property overridden depending on their related model properties (if they are part of a model). Refer to the model dialog and see also sceneObject.model.notMeasurable.
