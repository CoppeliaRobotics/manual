Detectable objects

Detectable objects are scene objects that can be detected by proximity sensors. They include:

  • Dummies
  • Shapes
  • OC trees
  • Point clouds

    • Dummies and point clouds, can not be detected by ray-type or randomized-type proximity sensors.

    Detectable objects can have their detectable property individually enabled or disabled. This can be set in the scene object common properties or through sceneObject.detectable.

    Collections are also detectable, since they might contain detectable objects.

    Additionally, detectable objects can have their detectable property overridden depending on their related model properties (if they are part of a model). Refer to the model dialog and see also sceneObject.model.notDetectable.
